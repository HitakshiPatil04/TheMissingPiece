# Case 001 — Research and accuracy notes

## Scope

The game is an educational reconstruction of the Great Canadian Maple Syrup Heist, associated with Quebec's strategic maple syrup reserve and events reported in 2011–2012. Public reports describe a large-scale theft, barrels that were empty or contained water, and a distribution/resale operation. Reported totals and values vary by source and are rounded in the game.

- CBC News — search for reporting on the Quebec maple syrup theft and court outcomes: https://www.cbc.ca/search?q=maple%20syrup%20heist
- The Canadian Press — search for case coverage and sentencing/conviction reports: https://www.thecanadianpressnews.ca/search/?q=maple%20syrup%20heist

## Accuracy and ethics notes

- The game uses rounded, commonly reported estimates (approximately 2,700 tonnes and approximately C$18 million at the time); published accounts may differ.
- Do not invent quotes or present the game's prompts as actual witness or suspect statements.
- The people named in the case are real. Keep descriptions factual, limited, and tied to publicly reported legal outcomes.
- A person's access, job, relationship, or presence is not proof of guilt by itself.
- The game mechanics simplify a complex case for learning. They are not a legal analysis or a complete representation of the trial record.
- Before publishing a public version, verify every biographical and legal detail against reliable sources and add direct citations next to case claims in the UI.
