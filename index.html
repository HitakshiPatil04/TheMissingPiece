Archive / Case 001

The reserve job.

Quebec, Canada · 2011–2012

A massive quantity of maple syrup disappeared from a strategic reserve. The scheme went unnoticed for months. Now, reconstruct the operation from the evidence.

Investigator's note: This experience combines documented historical facts with a reconstructed puzzle. The suspect profiles, evidence sequence, and deductions are presented for gameplay; they are not actual interview transcripts or a complete reproduction of the police file.
Incident report

A very sticky disappearance.

In 2012, an inventory inspection at Quebec's strategic maple syrup reserve uncovered barrels that were empty or filled with water. Investigators eventually traced a large theft involving insiders, transport, and resale.
